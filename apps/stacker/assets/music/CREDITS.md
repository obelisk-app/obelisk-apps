# Music credits

The three `*-ncc.mp3` tracks come from
[TETRA](https://github.com/soyezequiel/tetris-para-luna-negra) by
**soyezequiel**, a La Crypta hackathon project. That repo marks its own
royalty-free, Suno-generated tracks with the `ncc` prefix
(`ROYALTY_FREE_PREFIX` in its `src/audio/music.ts`). The game UI credits them
and links back to the source.

Only the `ncc` tracks are ours to ship. The rest of that folder holds
commercial recordings, and no prefix or folder makes those redistributable.
Carried over from obelisk-dex `docs/games.md` § Stacker audio.
